1

BCA Architects & Engineers

Good match for you

BCA is an architecture and engineering firm that’s been around since 1970. They have 100+ people across seven offices in New York, including one in Ithaca, and architects, interior designers, engineers and construction managers all work in-house.

A lot of their work is schools, colleges and healthcare. Their interiors team does the bright, colorful kind of spaces you see in these photos.

They do
Architecture, interior design, building and civil engineering
Projects
K–12, higher ed, healthcare, civil, community
Ithaca office
798 Cascadilla St, Suite C

2

HOLT Architects

Good match for you

HOLT has been based in Ithaca for over 60 years, with a second office in Syracuse. They describe themselves as having a process, not a style. Each project starts by listening to the client and the people who’ll actually use the building.

They work on campuses, housing, healthcare and offices, and do a lot of sustainable design (LEED, WELL, Passive House, net zero). If you’ve studied in Uris Library or eaten at Sage Hall, you’ve been inside their work.

They do
Architecture, planning, interior design, sustainable design, wayfinding and branding
Projects
Higher ed, housing, healthcare, workplace
Ithaca office
619 W State St

3

Chiang O’Brien Architects

Good match for you

Chiang O’Brien is a downtown Ithaca firm, a couple of blocks from the Commons, that mostly designs for colleges and universities. They’re a New York State certified minority- and woman-owned firm.

Their Cornell projects include Cornell Health, Jordan Hall and the Engaged Cornell Hub in Kennedy Hall. They won two AIA Southern New York awards in 2024–25.

They do
Programming studies, renovations, additions, new buildings
Projects
Mostly higher ed
Ithaca office
217 N Aurora St
chiangobrien.com ↗ Selected work ↗

TentativeWed 11/11 or 11/18 · 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

4

Sedgwick Business Interiors

Good match for you

Sedgwick is a commercial furniture dealer and a MillerKnoll Certified Dealer, so think Herman Miller and Knoll, plus DIRTT and a lot of other brands. Their team plans spaces, picks furniture and finishes, and handles delivery and install for offices, schools and hospitals around Upstate New York.

If you’re curious about the product and workplace side of interiors, this is the visit.

They do
Space planning, furniture and finishes, DIRTT walls, installation and project management
Projects
Offices, schools, healthcare, government
Ithaca showroom
21 Dutch Mill Rd
